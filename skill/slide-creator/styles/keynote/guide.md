# Keynote style

Dark by default, with a light option. Use it for talks, town halls and announcements presented live. The large type and broad colour pages carry one idea at a time.

Write a short conclusion as each slide title. Prefer a single number, statement, chart or diagram. Use `cover`, `chapter` and `statement` to pace the talk; put supporting detail in speaker notes. Keep tables rare and short. The showcase demonstrates the full vocabulary, but a real keynote should be much sparser than that test deck.

The default dark scheme uses a teal cover and warm accent. The `light` scheme keeps the same hierarchy on a pale canvas. Use `--scheme light` to select it. Use Windows/Office fonts and the tokens in `style.json`; do not add colours or font declarations to the deck.
